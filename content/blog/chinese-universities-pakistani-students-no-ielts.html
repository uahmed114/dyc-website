One of the biggest barriers stopping Pakistani students from studying abroad is the IELTS requirement. Preparing for the exam costs time and money — and a low score can close doors to universities in the UK, US, and Australia.

China is different.

The majority of English-taught degree programs at Chinese universities do not require IELTS or TOEFL. For Pakistani students who completed their O and A-levels in English, or who studied in an English-medium school, this requirement is often waived entirely.

In this guide, we break down the top 10 Chinese universities actively accepting Pakistani students without IELTS — covering programs, annual fees, scholarship availability, and what you need to apply instead.

Do Any Chinese Universities Really Accept Students Without IELTS?

Yes — and it is not a loophole or a compromise on quality. Chinese universities operate under a different admissions framework to Western institutions. For international students, most universities accept one of the following as proof of English proficiency instead of IELTS:

  • A certificate or letter from your previous school confirming that your medium of instruction was English
  • Completion of O-levels or A-levels (which are inherently English-medium)
  • An interview conducted in English by the admissions office
  • Completion of a foundation or preparatory year at the university

This means that for most Pakistani students — particularly those from English-medium schools — IELTS is simply not required.

That said, requirements vary by university and by program. Some medical programs, joint-degree courses, or highly competitive scholarships may request English proficiency documentation. Drop Your Case advises every student individually on exactly what their chosen university requires.

The Top 10 Chinese Universities for Pakistani Students Without IELTS

1. Wuhan University (WHU)

Location: Wuhan, Hubei
Annual Tuition: $3,000 – $5,000
Top Programs: MBBS, Law, Economics, Computer Science, Chinese Studies
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, WHU International Student Scholarship

Wuhan University consistently ranks among China’s top 10 universities and is one of the most popular destinations for Pakistani students — particularly those pursuing MBBS. The university has a large, established Pakistani student community and does not require IELTS for most English-taught programs when students provide proof of English-medium schooling.

2. Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)

Location: Wuhan, Hubei
Annual Tuition: $3,500 – $5,500
Top Programs: Medicine, Engineering, Computer Science, Management
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, HUST Merit Scholarship

HUST is one of China’s leading research universities, particularly strong in science and engineering. Pakistani students applying for MBBS and engineering programs regularly gain admission without IELTS. The university accepts English-medium school certificates as a substitute.

3. Beijing Jiaotong University (BJTU)

Location: Beijing
Annual Tuition: $3,000 – $4,500
Top Programs: Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Business, Economics
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, CPEC Scholarship, BJTU Merit Award

BJTU is one of the top universities under China’s CPEC scholarship program, making it a particularly strong choice for Pakistani students. Located in Beijing, it offers English-taught programs across engineering and business without requiring IELTS for students from English-medium backgrounds.

4. Shanghai University

Location: Shanghai
Annual Tuition: $3,500 – $5,000
Top Programs: MBA, Business Administration, Fine Arts, Engineering
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, Shanghai Municipal Scholarship

Shanghai University offers a wide range of English-taught postgraduate programs, including a well-regarded MBA program that has attracted Pakistani students in increasing numbers. No IELTS is required for applicants who completed prior studies in English.

5. Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT)

Location: Harbin, Heilongjiang
Annual Tuition: $3,000 – $5,000
Top Programs: Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace, Computer Science
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, HIT International Excellence Scholarship

HIT is one of China’s elite “985 Project” universities and is especially strong in engineering disciplines. It is a popular destination for Pakistani students pursuing civil and mechanical engineering. IELTS is not required for most programs when English-medium schooling is demonstrated.

6. Zhejiang University

Location: Hangzhou, Zhejiang
Annual Tuition: $4,000 – $6,000
Top Programs: Chinese Language, Medicine, Engineering, Agriculture
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, Confucius Institute Scholarship, ZJU Scholarship

Zhejiang University is one of China’s oldest and most prestigious institutions, consistently ranking in China’s top 5. It offers Confucius Institute scholarships for Chinese language programs — no IELTS required — and accepts proof of English-medium education for other programs.

7. Nanjing University of Science and Technology (NUST)

Location: Nanjing, Jiangsu
Annual Tuition: $2,500 – $4,000
Top Programs: Engineering, Computer Science, Materials Science
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, NUST International Scholarship

Not to be confused with Pakistan’s NUST, China’s NUST is a strong engineering university that consistently welcomes Pakistani students. Tuition fees are among the more affordable on this list, and IELTS is not required for English-taught programs.

8. Central South University (CSU)

Location: Changsha, Hunan
Annual Tuition: $3,000 – $5,000
Top Programs: MBBS, Mining Engineering, Materials Science, Business
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, CSU President Scholarship

Central South University has a well-established MBBS program that is WHO and PMC recognised — critical for Pakistani medical students who plan to return home and practise. The university accepts English-medium school certificates in place of IELTS and has a growing Pakistani student community.

9. Xi’an Jiaotong University (XJTU)

Location: Xi’an, Shaanxi
Annual Tuition: $3,500 – $5,500
Top Programs: Medicine, Engineering, Economics, Management
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, Silk Road Scholarship, XJTU Merit Award

XJTU is one of China’s original top universities and has strong ties to Pakistan through the CPEC corridor and Silk Road scholarship programs. It accepts English-medium schooling certificates as IELTS alternatives and is particularly strong for students pursuing medicine and engineering.

10. Jilin University

Location: Changchun, Jilin
Annual Tuition: $2,500 – $4,000
Top Programs: MBBS, Law, Chemistry, Automotive Engineering
Scholarships: CSC Scholarship, Jilin University Scholarship

Jilin University is one of China’s largest universities by enrollment and offers some of the most affordable tuition fees among top-ranked institutions. It has a long track record of accepting Pakistani students into its MBBS and engineering programs without IELTS requirements.

What Do You Need Instead of IELTS?

While each university has slightly different requirements, Pakistani students applying without IELTS will typically need to provide:

  • English Medium Certificate — A letter from your school or board confirming that your education was conducted in English
  • Academic Transcripts — Matric and intermediate certificates (or equivalent)
  • Passport Copy — Valid for at least 18 months beyond your intended start date
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP) — A personal statement outlining your academic goals
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Bank statement — Showing sufficient funds for tuition and living expenses

Which Scholarships Are Available at These Universities?

All 10 universities on this list offer access to one or more of the major scholarship programs available to Pakistani students:

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) — Fully funded, covering tuition, accommodation, and a monthly stipend. Highly competitive but available at all 10 universities listed above.

CPEC Scholarship — Dedicated to Pakistani students as part of the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor agreement. Covers tuition and living costs at selected universities.

Confucius Institute Scholarship — Primarily for Chinese language and culture programs. Available at several universities on this list including Zhejiang University.

University-Specific Scholarships — Most universities offer their own merit-based partial or full scholarships for high-achieving international students.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get into a top Chinese university without IELTS? Yes. All 10 universities listed in this guide accept Pakistani students without IELTS, provided you can demonstrate English-medium prior education.

What if my school was Urdu-medium? You may still be eligible — some universities offer foundation or preparatory year programs, and others conduct English interviews to assess proficiency. Contact DYC and we will advise based on your specific situation.

Is studying in China without IELTS recognised back in Pakistan? Yes. Degrees from HEC-recognised Chinese universities are fully valid in Pakistan regardless of whether IELTS was required for admission. The HEC recognition relates to the institution and degree, not the admissions process.

Which is the best city in China for Pakistani students? Wuhan and Beijing have the largest Pakistani student communities. Wuhan is particularly popular for MBBS students while Beijing attracts engineering and business students. Living costs are generally lower in cities like Changsha, Harbin, and Xi’an compared to Beijing and Shanghai.
